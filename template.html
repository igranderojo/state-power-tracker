Generation data: U.S. Energy Information Administration, Net Generation by State by Type of Producer by Energy Source, 1990–2024 (Total Electric Power Industry). Released September 2025; next EIA release expected October 2026. Fossil = coal, natural gas, petroleum, other gases. Renewable = conventional hydroelectric, wind, solar thermal/PV, geothermal, wood and other biomass. Nuclear tracked separately as carbon-free but not renewable.

Goal status: compiled from NCSL's state RPS/CES brief and state statutes; cross-checked against DSIRE and Environment America's 100%-clean-electricity tracking as of mid-2026. "Mandatory 100%" = a binding statute requiring 100% renewable or carbon-free electricity by a stated year. "Partial RPS/CES" = a binding standard below 100%. "Voluntary goal" = a stated but non-binding target. "No goal" = no RPS, CES, or clean-energy goal currently in statute.

Forecast (dashed, through 2032): modeled, not EIA data. Each state's renewable-share forecast is a logistic (S-curve) fit to that state's own actual history where the trend supports it — the ceiling, steepness, and inflection year all come from the data, never from a state's legal mandate. States without a stable curve fall back to a bounded linear projection instead of a fabricated curve. Nuclear share uses a damped historical trend; fossil share is always the remainder. A state's forecast can and sometimes does fall short of its own legal mandate — that is a finding, not an error.
